# Vision

Build a modern SaaS platform whose first vertical is veterinary management in
Paraguay.

The product combines a reusable business Core with vertical-specific Veterinary
capabilities and a reusable branding/theming layer so each product or tenant can
present its own visual identity without forking the application.

## Product principle

Optimize in this order:

1. data integrity;
2. tenant isolation;
3. clinical usability;
4. fast daily operation;
5. fiscal correctness;
6. auditability;
7. UX;
8. performance;
9. extensibility;
10. additional features.

## Reusable Core

Future verticals should reuse stable capabilities such as:

- customers;
- scheduling;
- catalog;
- inventory;
- purchasing;
- sales;
- payments;
- cash;
- billing;
- fiscal;
- notifications;
- files;
- audit.

Verticals must not force the Core into a generic EAV model.

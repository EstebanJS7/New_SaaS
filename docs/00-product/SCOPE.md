# MVP Scope

## Included

Platform:

- SaaS multi-tenant.
- Branch-ready architecture.
- RBAC.
- Entitlements/feature flags.
- typed tenant settings;
- Staff app.
- Customer portal.
- Product-level brand presets.
- Tenant branding overrides (logo, favicon, semantic colors, radius and approved
  typography).
- Shared staff/portal theming through semantic design tokens.

Veterinary:

- customers/guardians;
- patients;
- species/breeds;
- Patient 360;
- clinical encounters;
- vaccinations and plans;
- deworming;
- treatments;
- studies;
- weight history;
- attachments.

Operations:

- visual agenda;
- working hours and blocks;
- appointment statuses;
- portal booking;
- reminders;
- catalog;
- tax rates;
- warehouses;
- stock ledger;
- transfers;
- suppliers;
- purchases;
- POS/sales;
- mixed payments;
- cash opening/movements/closing;
- invoices;
- fiscal provider integration;
- KuDE/XML through provider capability;
- dashboards/basic reports;
- CSV/XLSX customer/patient imports;
- audit.
- explicit reversal/correction flows;
- reproducible synthetic demo tenant/seed;

## Explicit non-goals for initial MVP

- hospitalization;
- advanced surgery/anesthesia;
- external laboratory integrations;
- full accounting/general ledger;
- bank reconciliation;
- advanced accounts receivable;
- marketing CRM/campaigns;
- loyalty;
- native mobile apps;
- telemedicine;
- clinical AI;
- public API;
- public webhooks;
- marketplace;
- generic report builder;
- arbitrary tenant CSS/JavaScript injection;
- full page-builder/website builder;
- per-tenant custom frontend forks;
- advanced custom domains (architecture prepared, later phase);
- microservices.

Anything not required by MVP acceptance belongs to backlog unless explicitly
approved.
